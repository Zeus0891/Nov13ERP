
# 🟦 **MODULE 18 — ZERO LOSS (Max 10 tables)**

 Detect, prevent, audit, and analyze material loss across projects, warehouses, trucks, and job sites.

This module is **independent from Inventory**, but connects tightly to:

* InventoryTransactions
* InventoryLossEvents
* Projects
* DailyLogs
* Users
* Procurement / Receipts

---

## **1. ZeroLossEvent** *(PARENT)*

The main loss incident record.
Represents any detected loss of materials, tools, consumables, equipment parts, or inventory discrepancies.

Examples:

* Missing copper spools
* Lost drill batteries
* Lumber shrink
* Jobsite theft
* Supplier short shipment
* Internal misplacement

**Children:**

* ZeroLossItem
* ZeroLossInvestigation
* ZeroLossCorrectiveAction
* ZeroLossAttachment
* ZeroLossHistoryEvent

---

## **2. ZeroLossItem** *(CHILD)*

Specific items involved in the loss event.

Includes:

* itemId
* expected quantity
* actual quantity
* delta (lost amount)
* source (warehouse, truck, job site, PO, transfer, etc.)

---

## **3. ZeroLossCause**

Categorización de causa raíz:

Examples:

* Theft
* Damage
* Misplacement
* Poor storage
* Incorrect receiving
* Administrative error
* Vendor shorting

(Used for analytics + reporting.)

---

## **4. ZeroLossInvestigation** *(CHILD)*

Formal investigation record for each loss event.

Contains:

* investigator
* interviews
* related tasks
* comments
* initial hypothesis & final conclusion

**May reference:**

* Daily logs
* Project tasks
* Inventory transfers

---

## **5. ZeroLossCorrectiveAction** *(CHILD)*

Actions taken to prevent recurrence.

Examples:

* Lock materials cage
* Change material flow process
* Vendor warning
* Issue tool tags
* Redesign storage layout

Includes: responsible user, due date, and completion status.

---

## **6. ZeroLossAudit**

Periodic or automated audits triggered to check for discrepancies.

Types:

* warehouse audit
* jobsite audit
* return audit
* truck audit
* pre-closeout audit

---

## **7. ZeroLossAuditLine** *(CHILD)*

Item-by-item audit results for the audit.

Includes:

* expected qty
* counted qty
* variance
* root cause guess

---

## **8. ZeroLossAlert**

Automatic alerts generated by system heuristics:

Examples:

* “High-risk item missing from truck 7”
* “Variance detected in electrical materials”
* “Inventory count variance exceeds threshold”
* “Vendor receipt mismatch detected”

---

## **9. ZeroLossThreshold**

Configurable rules for loss detection:

* variance % thresholds
* high-risk SKUs
* project-level risk indicators
* automatic audit triggers

---

## **10. ZeroLossHistoryEvent** *(CHILD)*

Audit trail of all actions related to loss events:

* event created
* detection triggered
* investigation assigned
* root cause found
* corrective action completed

---

# 🔥 How Zero Loss Integrates Across ERP

### ✔ With Inventory

* Automatically creates a **ZeroLossEvent** when InventoryCount variance is detected
* Flags high-risk items in InventoryItem
* Ties to InventoryTransactions for traceability

### ✔ With Procurement

* Detect vendor short-shipment
* Auto-create ZeroLossEvent from PO Receipt variance

### ✔ With Projects

* Detect jobsite shrink
* Validate task material usage
* Integrate with Daily Logs for jobsite accountability

### ✔ With Field Service / Work Orders

* Tools lost during service calls
* Consumables not returned to truck

### ✔ With Analytics

* Identify patterns:

  * which projects lose more
  * which crews have higher shrink
  * which vendors short more shipments
